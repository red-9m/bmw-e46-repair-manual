# Blower Components

> Source: Bentley *BMW 3 Series (E46) 1999–2005 (M3) Repair Manual*, section 640-4 (PDF pages 1145–1148 of 1252).

## Blower motor, removing and installing

Remove housing for interior ventilation microfilter.

- Remove upper cover and microfilter.
- Open wiring harness loom (**A**) and remove wires.
- Unfasten screws (**B**) and remove lower housing.

![Microfilter housing at the rear of the engine compartment: wiring harness loom (A) and housing screws (B) — same illustration as in other chapters' microfilter service photos. Illustration ID: 0021314](e46_640-4_fig01.jpg)

Remove heater bulkhead cover:

- Remove engine compartment side trim panel. Turn locking clips (**A**) and slide trim panel up.
- Remove heater bulkhead cover mounting screws (**B**) and lift cover off bulkhead.

![Heater bulkhead cover: locking clips (A) on the side trim panel and mounting screws (B, white arrow at top). Illustration ID: 0021460](e46_640-4_fig02.jpg)

Remove intake manifold cover:

- Remove plastic trim covers (arrows).
- Remove cover hold down bolts and lift off cover.

![Intake manifold cover: plastic trim covers (white arrows) at the hold down points. Illustration ID: 0021021](e46_640-4_fig03.jpg)

Working inside right side of cowl, pull up on lever (arrow) and detach right air intake flap from blower housing.

![Right air intake flap lever (white arrow) inside the right side of the cowl. Illustration ID: 0022780](e46_640-4_fig04.jpg)

Pry off blower cover retaining clips (**A**). Remove screw (**B**) and lift off outer blower housing cover.

![Blower housing cover: retaining clips (A, white arrows) and screw (B, white arrow at top). Illustration ID: 0022781](e46_640-4_fig05.jpg)

Remove blower cover retaining screws (arrows). Lift off cover.

![Blower cover retaining screws (black arrows) with the blower fan visible under the cover. Illustration ID: 0022444](e46_640-4_fig06.jpg)

Detach electrical harness connector (arrow).

![Blower motor electrical harness connector (white arrow) at the blower motor — top part of a photo split across a page break. Continues in next image.](e46_640-4_fig07.jpg)

![Blower motor connector photo — bottom sliver: cowl area below the connector. Illustration ID: 0022443](e46_640-4_fig08.jpg)

Pry off blower motor retaining strap.

- Pull blower motor forward to remove.

![Hand pulling the blower motor forward out of the housing after prying off the retaining strap. Illustration ID: 0022441](e46_640-4_fig09.jpg)

- Installation is reverse of removal:
    - Align slot in blower motor with mounting tab in blower motor housing.

## Blower motor resistor pack, replacing

**Note:**

*The blower motor resistor pack is also referred to as the final stage unit.*

- Remove right footwell air duct as described earlier.

Working under right side of dashboard at side of IHKA housing, unhook linkage, then remove air distribution motor (arrow).

![Air distribution motor with its Linkage at the side of the IHKA housing (black arrow at motor, white arrow at linkage) — same illustration as in 640-2. Illustration ID: 0022959](e46_640-4_fig10.jpg)

To remove blower resistor pack from IHKA housing:

- Remove mounting screws (arrows).
- Detach electrical harness connector.
- Press retaining clip away from switch to release switch.

![Blower resistor pack at the side of the IHKA housing — top part of a photo split across a page break: resistor pack (white arrow). Continues in next image.](e46_640-4_fig11.jpg)

![Blower resistor pack photo — bottom part: mounting screw (white arrow, left) and harness connector (white arrow, top). Illustration ID: 0022450](e46_640-4_fig12.jpg)

- Installation is reverse of removal.
    - Make sure electrical harness is routed as before.
    - Align guides and clips correctly.
