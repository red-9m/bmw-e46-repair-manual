# General (Transfer Case)

> Source: BMW 3 Series (E46) Repair Manual, section 270-1, Transfer Case chapter (PDF pages 470 of 1252).

This repair group covers removal and installation of the all wheel drive transfer case. Internal transfer case repair is not covered. Special press tools and procedures are required to disassemble and service the internal geartrain.

Some procedures covered in this repair group will require you to refer to the following repair groups:

- [180 Exhaust System]
- [260 Driveshaft]

The transfer case is used in all wheel drive models to direct power from the transmission to both the front and rear differentials via driveshafts. This transfer case is unique in that it delivers 38% of the transmission's torque output to the front differential, and 62% of the torque to the rear. Unlike the transfer cases used in many four wheel drive trucks, the transfer case used is a single range unit, permanently engaged to drive all four wheels all of the time.
