E46 Repair Manual — Index

A section-by-section guide to the Bentley BMW 3 Series (E46) 1999–2005 Repair Manual, including M3 models. The manual covers routine maintenance, troubleshooting, and repair of the engine, drivetrain, suspension, steering, brakes, body, electrical systems, heating and air conditioning, and safety equipment.

Use the topic overview to find a repair area, then the section table to open its HTML page. Section IDs match the printed manual; page ranges refer to the source PDF.
